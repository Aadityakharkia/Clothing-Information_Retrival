CASE 1 · EXACT PHRASE VS VSM

"cotton shirt" Filter

VSM gives non-zero scores to garments where "cotton" and "shirt" appear separately. Exact phrase search requires pos(shirt)=pos(cotton)+1, isolating genuine shirts only.

CASE 2 · PROXIMITY VS VSM

winter WITHIN/3 wear

"wear" appears across almost every Indian clothing description, so VSM scores dozens of non-winter garments. Positional k=3 constraint restricts to genuine winter garments (Jackets, Hoodies, Sweatshirts).
